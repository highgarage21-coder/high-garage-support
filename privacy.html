HIGH GARAGE Privacy Policy Updated: October 5, 2026 Contact: highgarage21@gmail.com

HIGH GARAGE has no app-operated server, advertising SDK or analytics SDK. Language selection and saved media titles and URLs are stored locally on your device. Swipe left in My media to remove saved items.

Websites opened in the browser and media hosts receive network requests including your IP address and requested resource. They may use cookies and collect information according to their own policies. HIGH GARAGE does not provide anonymous browsing or control those services.

Use Settings → Clear browser data to delete WebKit website cookies, caches and stored website data. This may sign you out of websites. Your saved media and language choice remain. Removing the app removes local app-container data. Device backups are managed through iOS.

The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts or location access. Opening an email to support shares only the information you choose to send. Support messages received at the contact address are used to respond and resolve the issue; do not send passwords or confidential media links. Support correspondence is kept while needed to resolve the request, unless applicable obligations require otherwise. Contact us to request deletion of correspondence.

Third-party sites, media services and AirPlay destinations are governed by their own policies. No video catalogue is provided by HIGH GARAGE.
