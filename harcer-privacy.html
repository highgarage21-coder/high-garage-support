Harcer Garage Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026. This policy covers Harcer Garage version 1.0, the offline Armenian team word game developed by Narek Ter-Hovhannisyan.

Data used by the app

Harcer Garage does not collect or transmit personal data. It has no accounts, advertising, tracking, analytics SDKs, online multiplayer, or in-app purchases. The app does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photos.

Game state

Team names, game settings, scores and the current round are held in memory on your device. They are not sent to the developer or any server. The current match is not saved as an online profile and may reset when the app is fully closed.

Support email

If you voluntarily email highgarage21@gmail.com, we receive the email address, message and attachments you send. We use that information to answer your request and investigate the reported issue. This is separate from data collection by the app. Please do not send passwords or other sensitive information. To request deletion of a support conversation from our mailbox, contact the same address.

This website

These support pages do not include advertising or analytics scripts. The website hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process technical request information such as IP addresses to operate and secure the service. See GitHub's privacy statement for its practices.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if the app's data practices change. For privacy questions, contact Narek Ter-Hovhannisyan at highgarage21@gmail.com.
